Version10 good-overlap diagnostic protocol
================================================
1. Report Var(w_obs | Z=0) [var_observed_control]
2. Report control-weight CV [weight_cv_control]
3. Report original variance ratio Var(w_ideal)/Var(w_obs) [original_variance_ratio]
4. Label VBM R2 unstable when the denominator is near zero or the ratio is extreme [flag_good_overlap_instability]
5. Use stabilized ratio only as supplementary numerical evidence [stabilized_variance_ratio]
